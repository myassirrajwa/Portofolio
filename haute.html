HauteView

The HauteView project is a mobile e-commerce app I built using Flutter (Dart), driven by the design principle of creating the fastest possible shopping experience, addressing common e-commerce pain points like slow loading times, complex navigation, and difficulty getting support when issues arise. As a designer and developer, my primary focus is ensuring that the philosophy of speed and fast response is applied at every step, from product discovery to post-purchase issue resolution.
