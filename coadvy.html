Coadvy

I developed the Coadvy mobile app project using the Flutter (Dart) framework with the primary goal of creating a highly efficient and responsive coffee ordering experience for users, directly addressing pain points such as long queues during peak hours and slow order processing. As the sole UI/UX Designer and Developer on the project, I focused on designing a flow that minimized friction; my design presented the checkout process in just three short steps and integrated a prominent Order Again feature on the homepage to allow loyal users to reorder their favorites in seconds, effectively cutting transaction times by over 50%. To deliver on the "fast-response" promise, I ensured fast application performance through an optimal implementation of Flutter state management, and most importantly, I integrated a Real-time Chat Support feature that is always accessible on the order status page. This chat interface is designed to be minimalist and includes a quick reply option to make it easy for users to report issues such as delayed orders or incorrect products without having to type extensively.
